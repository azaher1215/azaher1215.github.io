Robotics · DRONES Lab, University at Buffalo

Navigation stack for Spot and Jackal

The goal
Make it easy to compare robot navigation setups without rebuilding everything each time.
What I built
A modular ROS 2 pipeline for Boston Dynamics Spot and Clearpath Jackal. Sensing, SLAM (SLAM Toolbox, FAST-LIO, ORB-SLAM3, RTAB-Map), localization and Nav2 planning can each be swapped out.
How it was tested
The same missions, metrics and recordings across both robots, in simulation and on the real robot with LiDAR.
